What FocusCycle Guarantees

  • Correct phase after tab close and reload
  • Accurate time after laptop sleep (tested up to 24 h)
  • No double-skips or 00:00 stalls on wake
  • Mute preference survives page reloads
  • Deterministic from two numbers — start time + paused duration

Known Limits

  • Single browser profile on one device only — no cross-device sync in v1
  • State is lost if browser storage is cleared
  • Sleep longer than 24 h may cause minor phase drift
  • Requires the browser to stay installed (not a native app)

How It Works

FocusCycle stores a wall-clock sessionStart timestamp and a cumulative totalPausedMs counter in localStorage. On every resume — tab reload, sleep/wake, or discard — it recomputes your exact position in the 25/5 cycle from those two numbers and Date.now(). The setInterval only refreshes the display; it is never the source of truth.
